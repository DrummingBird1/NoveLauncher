# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels and languages —
  this project is used and discussed in seven of them.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project
space, and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **solvaris2@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately and
in confidence. Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that
violate this policy, and may block repeat offenders from participating.
