Private by construction

Not a promise in a policy document — an architectural choice.

  • Nothing is collected. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting in shipped builds, no account, no ads.
  • Usage data stays local. Statistics live in a database on your device and are used only to rank your apps.
  • One network call, and it's optional. The weather widget sends approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo — only if you grant location permission. Deny it and a default city is used.
  • Your backups are yours. Local, your own Google Drive, or your own NAS — encrypted with a password only you hold.
  • Auditable. The full source is published so you can verify all of the above yourself.
On app lock, honestly: the app lock, private folder and hidden apps are a deterrent, not a sandbox. A locked app is still reachable from recents, notifications, another launcher or ADB — true of every third-party launcher. For real isolation, use Android's built-in Work Profile.
